Homework 1 · Personal portfolio

A small site with a real history

The deliverable is a published portfolio repository with a clear home page and at least five meaningful commits that show progress—not repeated empty commits.

01 · Introduce

Tell your story

Add your name, short bio, photo with useful alternative text, and a technical-skills matrix.

02 · Curate

Show real projects

Link two or three course projects with concise context and working demos where available.

03 · Publish

Keep the history

Use at least five focused commits to record setup, content, styling, responsive work, and final fixes.

Submission checklist: repository is public, the Pages URL loads, all project links work, and the commit history contains five or more meaningful changes.
