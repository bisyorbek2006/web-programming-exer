How to Make Ramen

What You Need

  • 1 ramen packet
  • 500ml water
  • 1 egg
  • Green onion
  • Chili flakes

Steps

  1. Boil the water in a pot.
  2. Add the noodles and cook for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the seasoning packet and stir vigorously.
  4. Crack in the egg and wait 30 seconds.
  5. Serve immediately with toppings.
