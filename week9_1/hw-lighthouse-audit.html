Week 09 · Homework 01 · Accessibility

Audit the page. Improve the experience.

An accessibility review of the Week 9.1 learning pages, focused on landmarks, heading hierarchy, and meaningful announcements for people using assistive technology.

04 semantic and ARIA remediations

Audit method

Inspect structure, then verify the fix

The audit combines a Lighthouse accessibility pass with a manual review of document landmarks, heading order, control names, and feedback behavior. Automated results are a starting point; keyboard and screen-reader context still require human checks.

Scope: Week 9.1 sample pages and the shared learning-page structure. The report records four representative semantic and ARIA improvements; it does not claim that a single automated score covers every accessibility need.

Findings & remediations

Four changes that add useful context

01 · Landmarks

Give page regions meaning

Use a single main landmark, semantic header and navigation regions, and a footer for supporting information so assistive-technology users can move by page region.

02 · Headings

Make the outline predictable

Keep one descriptive level-one heading, then organize sections under level-two headings and related content under level three.

03 · Controls

Name interactive elements

Prefer native buttons and explicitly associated labels. Give icon-only controls an accessible name instead of relying on their visual symbol.

04 · Feedback

Announce form status

Associate instructions and error text with fields, and use a polite live region for status updates that appear after interaction.

Verification checklist

Check more than the score

  • Navigate the page using only Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, and Space; confirm focus remains visible.
  • Inspect the heading outline and landmark list with browser accessibility tools.
  • Confirm every form field has a programmatic label and that errors can be located with the field.
  • Re-run Lighthouse after remediation and document findings without treating the score as a complete audit.
